Privacy Policy

Paperly: PDF Scanner & Reader · Effective October 8, 2026

This policy explains what data the Android app Paperly: PDF Scanner & Reader (package com.dovdov.scan, "Paperly", "the app") handles and why. The app is published by Dovdov.dev ("we", "us"). If you have a question, write to dovdov.apps@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your scans, PDFs, recognized text and signatures are stored only on your device. We have no servers and never receive them.
  • Text recognition (OCR) and translation run on your device.
  • You don't need an account, and we don't use analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • Free users see ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device data to show and measure ads, as described below. Pro users get no ads, and the ads SDK isn't loaded for them.
  • We don't sell your personal data.

1. Camera

Paperly asks for camera access only to scan documents. The camera works only while the scanner is open. Captured images are processed on your device (edge detection, cropping, filters) and saved in the app's private storage. We don't receive them.

2. Your documents

Scans, imported photos, PDFs, recognized text, signatures, folders and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps can't read this storage, and the app turns off Android cloud backup for it. Your files leave the device only when you choose to share them, for example through the Android share sheet, or to export them to a location you pick.

When you import photos, the app uses the system Photo Picker and gets access only to the images you select. The app doesn't request storage permissions.

3. Text recognition and translation

Text recognition and language detection use Google ML Kit through Google Play services, and translation uses Google ML Kit. All of them process your text on your device; your document text isn't sent to us or to Google. Recognition and translation models may be downloaded from Google when first needed. By default, translation models download over Wi-Fi only. Like other Google Play services components, ML Kit may send Google limited diagnostic and usage information (such as performance and error data) that doesn't include your document contents. See Google's ML Kit terms.

4. Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data, such as app launches, taps and crash information.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before loading any ad. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings.

Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you buy Paperly Pro, the app doesn't load the ads SDK.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play. We don't receive your payment details. The app only receives the status of your purchase from Google Play so it can unlock Pro features and restore them on your devices.

6. Feedback

If you tap Send feedback, the app opens your own email app with the app version, device model and Android version filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. We use what you send only to answer you and to fix problems, and we delete it when it's no longer needed.

8. Keeping and deleting data

Your documents stay on your device until you delete them. Deleted files go to Recently deleted and are removed permanently after 30 days, or earlier if you choose "Delete forever". Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes all of its data from your device. To manage the data Google holds about ads, use your Google account and the Android advertising settings.

9. Children

Paperly isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, or to withdraw consent. Because we don't hold your documents or any account data, most requests are handled on your device (see section 8) or through Google for ads data. You can contact us at any time at dovdov.apps@gmail.com.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll publish the new version on this page and update the effective date. For important changes, we'll also let you know in the app or in the Google Play listing.
